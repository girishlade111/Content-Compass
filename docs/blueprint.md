# **App Name**: Content Compass

## Core Features:

- Content Ideation: Generate content ideas and titles using a generative AI tool, based on keywords related to the SaaS product and target audience.
- Editorial Calendar: Display a 3-month editorial calendar with content pieces, their target audience, and distribution channels.
- Performance Analytics: Provide a dashboard view of content performance metrics like views, shares, and conversions.
- Customer Profiling: Enable users to input target customer information, including pain points and demographics, for content tailoring.
- Channel Recommendations: Suggest optimal distribution channels based on the target audience and content type.
- Content Scheduling: Enable a drag-and-drop interface for scheduling content pieces within the editorial calendar.
- CTA Generation: AI tool suggesting relevant calls-to-action to maximize conversions within the generated content.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Saturated blue (#4285F4) to convey trust and professionalism, aligning with the SaaS industry.
- Background color: Light blue (#E3F2FD), offering a clean and calming backdrop.
- Accent color: Vibrant purple (#9C27B0) for highlighting key elements and calls-to-action, adding a touch of creativity.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a sans-serif font for a modern and easily readable design.
- Use simple, professional icons to represent different content types and distribution channels.
- Maintain a clean, grid-based layout for the editorial calendar and dashboard, ensuring ease of navigation and content discoverability.
- Use subtle transitions and animations for interactive elements to enhance user engagement without being distracting.
